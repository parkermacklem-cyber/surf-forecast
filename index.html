About the forecasts

SWELL displays model-based marine and wind information. It does not currently calculate local breaking-wave heights, predict surf quality with a calibrated algorithm, or guarantee conditions at any beach.

The model data may be unavailable for some coastal coordinates. The app clearly shows missing data rather than inventing readings.
