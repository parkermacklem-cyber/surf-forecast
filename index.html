Forecast transparency

SWELL requests marine and atmospheric model data for the selected coordinates. Marine grid coverage varies, especially close to shore. If the data is unavailable, SWELL displays missing values instead of fabricating conditions.

A 30-minute refresh means the app checks for updated information; it does not mean the underlying weather and marine models publish new forecasts every 30 minutes.
